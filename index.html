About Tori

Hi, I'm Victoria Agyare, but everyone calls me Tori. I study Computer Science at Georgia State University in Atlanta and graduate in May 2028.

I build full-stack web apps, most recently as an SEO Tech Developer Intern, and I won 1st place in the Cox track at a hackathon with Quest. I'm also a Career Prep Tech Fellow with MLT and on the executive board of the Programming Club at GSU.

Outside of class I'm usually listening to Malcolm Todd, The Marías, Radiohead, 2hollis or Rommulas, scrolling Pinterest, looking for clothes on Vinted, or taking a nap. I love being outside in nature, and I'll always stop for a good sunset. Since my trip to New York, I'm also always on the hunt for good boba.

I've loved Apple for as long as I can remember, from the iPod to the old Aqua look. That's why this site is styled like apple.com from the early 2000s.
